Handcrafted Indie Games and Kinetic Dioramas

Samtagon is an independent boutique game studio founded by Sachin Yadav, dedicated to crafting intricate cellular systems, urban transit builders, and tactile arcade games. All games are engineered with high performance in modern C++17, Raylib, WebAssembly, and Godot Engine.

Interactive 3D Diorama Features:

  • Double-level crossover infinite track with stone arch viaduct and timber trestle bridge.
  • Dual rolling-stock trains: classic emerald steam locomotive and aerodynamic sky-blue high-speed bullet train.
  • Volumetric night lighting with headlights, illuminated carriage passenger windows, and sparkling viaduct gas lanterns.
  • Cozy atmospheric weather simulation including gentle rain precipitation and interactive pond water wake ripples.
  • Synthesized Web Audio sound design with authentic dual-chime steam whistle, rail click-clack rhythms, and acoustic hops.

Catalog of Featured Browser Games:

  • Metro Grid: 2.5D isometric urban transit tycoon and rail network builder.
  • Conway's Game of Life: Tactile mathematical cellular automata simulation engine.
  • Alien Blaster: 2D retro arcade action platformer with fluid projectile physics.
  • Godot Quest: Charming pixel adventure exploration RPG.
  • Snake & PingPong: Classic responsive arcade games with kinetic physics.
