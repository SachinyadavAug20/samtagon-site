Studio Philosophy

THE SAMTAGON MANIFESTO

Why we build games through low-level systems engineering, procedural audio, and autonomous loops.

1. The Supremacy of the Kinetic Loop

At Samtagon, we believe the best games are not passive narratives, but living kinetic simulations. When you play Metro Grid, you don't merely look at static art — you trigger an emergent, self-balancing infinite transit loop. Commuters make decisions based on deterministic math; signals space out trains via block separation; and procedural VVVF audio whines directly from frequency oscillators.

2. Zero Engine Bloat • Low-Level Respect

Modern game development has become overloaded with massive gigabyte-heavy runtimes and opaque garbage collectors. Samtagon pushes in the opposite direction: C++17, statically linked Raylib, handcrafted data structures, and procedural generation. Every byte of memory has a purpose. The result is pure, instantaneous 60 FPS responsiveness on desktop and WebAssembly alike.

3. 100% Procedural Synthesis

Why record static .wav files when sound is vibration and mathematics? In our flagship titles, audio is generated in real-time code — simulating the magnetic field resonance of three-phase electric traction motors (VVVF), pneumatic door releases, and harmonic chimes.
